RIPPLES REVIEWER SAMPLE DATA
D7 · Apps in Amazon Quick · the approval gate
draft-state model: DRAFT → APPROVED / REJECTED
automations (D5a/b/c) act only on APPROVED

AI-extracted LCCAP commitments with their source page. Edit any field to correct an extraction error, then Approve to trust the commitment or Reject to send it back. Approval moves the item DRAFT → APPROVED. Budget is a recorded commitment amount only — no money is handled anywhere in the system.

Follow-up letters (D4b), advisory guidance cards (D4a) and readiness reports awaiting approval — each shown beside its source record. Approve sets review_state = APPROVED, the exact flag the deadline-escalation (D5a), typhoon-playbook (D5b) and pre-season (D5c) automations read before they send, share or post. Nothing sends from this app.

Citizen report triage results (D4c) with matching-report context and a corroborate / reject control. Reporter identity is never shown here — there is no field for it (S5/CC-5). Corroborate is enabled only when the fixed rule is already met (≥2 matching reports in 30 days, or a satellite check) — a lone report cannot be hand-corroborated (S6/CC-6).

Sample data. This mockup reads the sample CSVs in data/ (projects.csv, advisories.csv, evidence.csv) and the DRAFT outputs of the D4a/b/c flows. Real city and office names are used only to show how the system works; nothing here describes the real status of any LGU, DPWH, DENR, MMDA or PAGASA record. This app is the approval gate — it only sets a draft's review_state; it never sends, shares, or publishes. Build spec: quick-build/D7-ripples-reviewer/app-spec.md.
