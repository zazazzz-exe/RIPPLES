Rules Engine Appendix B — real-risk & advisory levels precomputed into cities.csv now · app backend later
LowModerateHighCritical
Real-risk level = current hazard points + open-gap points. Advisory levels follow PAGASA thresholds. Deterministic and explainable — AI never sets a risk level, it only does the language work.
Governing principles
Rule-based where it matters
Risk & advisory levels from fixed rules — AI only extracts, triages & drafts. Every result explainable.
A person approves everything
Quick drafts; a reviewer approves. Nothing is sent or published automatically.
One space grounds everything
A single Quick space holds the doc, CSVs & LCCAP files so every component reads the same records.
