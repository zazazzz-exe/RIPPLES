For households

Secure your roof, windows and loose items, and prepare a go-bag. In Tinajeros and other riverside barangays, be ready to move early to Malabon National High School (1,500), Tonsuya covered court (400) or the City Hall annex (800). Follow your barangay's go-signal.

For schools

Prepare for class suspension and protect ground-floor equipment and records at riverside campuses. Any suspension follows DepEd and Malabon LGU announcements.

For farmers

Fishpond and sluice-gate operators along the Tullahan: close gates, secure nets and harvest stock that can be moved. Small boats remain in port.

For barangay officials

The Tullahan River wall repair is overdue: station a watcher at the 120-m sandbag gap and pre-position sandbags. The early-warning sensors need maintenance: keep manual watchers at the two offline sites.

Follow official warnings and evacuation orders from PAGASA and NDRRMC.

We write regarding the Tullahan River wall repair in Tinajeros, a commitment under Malabon City's LCCAP 2020–2030. The recorded budget is ₱120 million, with a committed deadline of May 2026. Our records show the status as Delayed, progress about 30%, and the commitment marked Overdue. 2 follow-ups recorded, 0 replies logged.

So that this record can be kept accurate and complete, we respectfully request:

  1. Current progress and a revised completion date.
  2. Interim measures your office recommends for the affected barangays in Tinajeros.
  3. A copy of the latest progress report, under Executive Order No. 2, s. 2016 (Freedom of Information) or the local FOI ordinance.

Your reply will be posted in full on the City Page. If no reply is received within 15 working days, the record will note "No reply" for that period.

Safeguards recap
  1. S1No all-clear claims. Official warnings come first; defer to and link PAGASA and NDRRMC.
  2. S2Every letter, card and report is a DRAFT until a person approves it. Demo sends go to test addresses only.
  3. S3Neutral status words only: overdue, delayed, needs maintenance, no reply.
  4. S4Risk and advisory levels come from fixed-rule lookups, never model scoring.
  5. S5No output shows who submitted a citizen report.
  6. S6A report stays unverified until two more matching reports or a satellite check corroborate it.
  7. S7Sample data is labeled everywhere it appears.
