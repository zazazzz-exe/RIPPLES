Record Ledger

A blockchain-style ledger for project records. Every commitment's record is fingerprinted with SHA-256 and chained to the block before it, so no one can quietly edit a past record. The ledger is for record integrity only: no currency, wallets or payments. Sample data.

How it works

  1. 1 · RecordA project's details, status, progress, deadline, maintenance and every piece of evidence.
  2. 2 · FingerprintThe record is hashed with SHA-256. Change one character and the fingerprint changes completely.
  3. 3 · BlockThe fingerprint goes into a new block together with the previous block's hash, linking the chain.
  4. 4 · VerifyAnyone can recompute the hashes in their own browser. An edited record or block breaks the chain.
