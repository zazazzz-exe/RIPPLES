Run the automations on sample data. Everything they produce is a draft; nothing is published or sent until it is approved on the Scorecard page, and sends go to a test outbox only.

Typhoon event playbook

A simulated typhoon crosses the country on the same track as the 3D demo. Each city gets a wind signal by its distance from the track, its real-risk level is recomputed by the fixed rules, and guidance cards, interim notices and follow-up letters are drafted.

Advisory guidance card

Turn one PAGASA or NDRRMC advisory into guidance for households, schools, farmers and barangay officials.

Deadline escalation

Selects projects past their deadline and not completed, or completed but needing maintenance, skipping any followed up in the last 30 days.

Pre-season readiness

Builds a readiness report per city from its open gaps, scorecard and the sample outlook. Approved reports are posted on the City Page and shared with the DRRMO, Sanggunian and local media (test addresses).
